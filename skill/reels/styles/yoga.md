# Yoga style (basic, adjust with the user)

- Length: 20-40 s, 5-8 clips.
- Each clip shows a pose or a flow whole: entering the pose, holding it, coming out. Never cut in the middle of a movement. Clips of 4-7 s.
- Normal speed.
- Soft fades between clips: `"transition": "fade"`, `"fadeSeconds": 0.5`.
- Calm music the user provides, `"clipVolume": 0` (room sound muted) and music volume 0.5. If the teacher speaks and the user wants the voice, set `"clipVolume": 1` and music volume 0.15.
- Calm accent colour, for example sage `#7FA88B` or sand `#C9A27E`, unless the user has a brand colour.
- Intro: 1-2 short cards (studio or teacher name, then class or event and date). Text stays short: a phone screen shows it for 2.5 s.
- Outro: what to do next ("Reserva tu plaza", "Primera clase gratis") plus the web or Instagram handle, and the poster or logo if there is one.
- Prefer clips with good light, the whole body in frame and a calm background. Skip clips where people walk across or the camera moves a lot.
