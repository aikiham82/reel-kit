# Aikido style

- Each technique shown whole: from the attack or grab to the fall and recovery. Clips of 4-9 s.
- Normal speed, never slow motion.
- Hard cuts: `"transition": "cut"`.
- Original sound with the falls, no music: `"clipVolume": 1`, no `music`.
- Close framing on the people doing the technique: `cut` with the default `--frame track`.
- Seminar promos: 2 intro cards (teacher name and rank, then dates and place), outro with the seminar poster and the website. Display font in orange `#F07A2E`.
- Reels for the teacher themself, not a promo: no intro, no outro, only techniques.
